The project does not change format as you gain control. Your agent, the Studio app, the browser editor, the
SDK, the Player, and the renderer all work from the same HTML project. Made it with your agent on a Mac?
[Open it in the Studio app](/studio-app/existing-project) and keep going there.

## Choose by the size of the change

Use the agent for changes to the story or several scenes. Use the browser editor, or point at the picture in the
Studio app, for the specific visual or timing detail you can see.
