You do not need to learn HyperFrames' internal workflow names. Open the situation that resembles yours, copy the request, and adjust it.

<Tip>
  First HyperFrames project? Follow [Make your first video](/quickstart) instead — it walks the whole path once. This page is the faster route when you already know what HyperFrames is.
</Tip>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A product, website, or launch brief" icon="rocket">
    **Best for** launch videos, product tours, promos, and showcases that should use the product's real interface.

    **Give the agent** a public URL or launch brief, the audience, the main promise, and any claims or screens that must appear.

    ```text
    /hyperframes Make a 45-second launch video for https://example.com.
    It is for small design teams. Show the real interface, focus on how quickly
    a team can go from an idea to a shared result, and end with “Try it together.”
    Keep the pacing confident rather than frantic.
    ```

    [Product and website workflow →](/guides/product-launch-video)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Notes, an article, or an idea" icon="lightbulb">
    **Best for** explainers where every visual is invented from the subject rather than captured from a product site.

    **Give the agent** the source text, who needs to understand it, the desired takeaway, and the maximum length.

    ```text
    /hyperframes Turn these notes into a 60-second visual explainer for a
    non-technical audience. The viewer should understand the three stages and
    why the second one prevents mistakes. Use diagrams and typography rather
    than stock footage.
    ```

    [Explainer workflow →](/guides/faceless-explainer)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Existing talking-head footage" icon="video">
    Choose by what must happen to the clip:

    | Desired result | Best route |
    | --- | --- |
    | Plain or expressive subtitles; footage otherwise untouched | Embedded captions |
    | Titles, quotes, lower thirds, data callouts, or side panels over untouched footage | Talking-head recut |
    | Remove pauses, reorder speech, replace shots, or change the underlying edit | Custom video |

    ```text
    /hyperframes Add clean word-level captions to this interview clip. Keep the
    footage and audio unchanged. Use high contrast and emphasize only the most
    important phrase in each sentence.
    ```

    [Captions and designed recuts →](/guides/captions-and-recuts)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A pull request" icon="code-pull-request">
    **Best for** a feature reveal, fix explanation, refactor summary, or team changelog built from a GitHub PR.

    **Give the agent** the PR link, the audience, the tone, and whether to emphasise user impact or implementation.

    ```text
    /hyperframes Turn https://github.com/example/repo/pull/123 into a concise
    feature reveal for product engineers. Lead with what changed for users,
    then show the two most important code decisions. Keep it under 60 seconds.
    ```

    [PR-to-video workflow →](/guides/pr-to-video)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A short motion-first idea" icon="wand-magic-sparkles">
    **Best for** motion-is-the-message pieces, usually under ten seconds, with no narration.

    **Give the agent** the exact text or data, dimensions, duration, visual tone, and whether the background must be transparent.

    ```text
    /hyperframes Create a six-second vertical motion graphic announcing
    “10 million renders.” Count up to the number, then reveal the label.
    Use bold editorial type and export with a transparent background.
    ```

    [Motion graphics workflow →](/guides/motion-graphics)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A music track" icon="music">
    **Best for** lyric videos, beat-synced slideshows, visualisers, and promos where the music sets the pacing.

    **Give the agent** the audio file, any lyrics, the media to cut, and the intended format.

    ```text
    /hyperframes Make a 30-second vertical promo driven by this music track.
    Cut the attached product stills on the major beats, reserve the chorus for
    the strongest image, and end on the supplied logo.
    ```

    [Music-to-video workflow →](/guides/music-to-video)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A presentation" icon="display">
    **Best for** discrete slides with fragment reveals, speaker notes, branching, presenter mode, or clickable navigation.

    Ask for a **slideshow** explicitly, so the result stays a navigable deck instead of becoming a rendered linear video.

    ```text
    /hyperframes Build a ten-slide interactive product briefing from these
    notes. Use progressive reveals, add speaker notes, and include a final
    branch where the presenter can open either the roadmap or pricing detail.
    ```

    [Slideshow workflow →](/guides/slideshow)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Something custom" icon="shapes">
    **Best for** longer or unusual work: multi-scene montages, title cards, static loops, mixed media, or projects where you want to co-create the structure with the agent.

    ```text
    /hyperframes Plan a 90-second brand reel from these three product films
    and this logo. Co-design the structure with me before building: propose a
    scene order and the feeling of each section, then wait for my approval.
    ```

    [Custom video workflow →](/guides/general-video)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## After you choose

Install the guidance once with `npx hyperframes skills update`, then send your request starting with `/hyperframes`. The agent confirms the creative direction before it builds, so correct the message, length, and format at that point rather than after a finished video exists.

When the first version opens, make direct visible edits in [Studio](/studio) and ask the agent for anything that affects the story, several scenes, or new media. Export from Studio's **Renders** panel. If something blocks you, start from the symptom in [Get unstuck](/help).

Writing a stronger request is its own short guide: [Give your agent a useful brief](/guides/prompting).
