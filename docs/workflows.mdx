You do not need to learn HyperFrames' internal workflow names. Pick the situation that resembles yours — each path tells you what to provide and gives you a request to copy.

<Tip>
  First HyperFrames project? Follow [Make your first video](/quickstart) instead. It walks the whole path once, end to end. This page is the faster route when you already know what HyperFrames is.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Working with footage? Choose by what must happen to the clip. **Subtitles only, footage untouched** → embedded captions. **Titles, quotes, lower thirds, or side panels over untouched footage** → a designed recut. **Reordering speech, removing pauses, or changing the edit itself** → a [custom video](/guides/general-video). The [captions and recuts guide](/guides/captions-and-recuts) covers the first two.
</Note>

## After you choose

Install the guidance once with `npx hyperframes skills update`, then start your request with `/hyperframes`. The agent confirms the creative direction before it builds, so correct the message, length, and format at that point — it is much cheaper than rebuilding a finished video around the wrong idea.

When the first version opens, make direct visible edits in [Studio](/studio), and ask the agent for anything that affects the story, several scenes, or new media. Export from Studio's **Renders** panel. If something blocks you, start from the symptom in [Get unstuck](/help).

Writing a stronger request is its own short guide: [Give your agent a useful brief](/guides/prompting).
