---
title: "Create a faceless explainer"
description: "Turn notes, an article, or a difficult idea into a visual explanation without product footage."
---

Use a faceless explainer when the source is an idea or piece of text—not a product website or existing talking-head video.

The agent invents the visual language from typography, diagrams, abstract graphics, data visualization, icons, and generated media.

## Start with the point

```text
/hyperframes Create a 60-second explainer from the attached notes.
The audience is new product managers. Explain why confidence intervals
matter without assuming a statistics background. Calm, editorial, and
diagram-led. End with one practical rule they can remember.
```

Include:

- the source text or topic;
- what the audience already knows;
- the one idea they should remember;
- the desired length and format;
- whether narration is wanted;
- claims or examples that must remain exact.

## Review the explanation

Check the storyboard before judging visual polish:

1. Does the opening establish the question?
2. Does each scene add one useful idea?
3. Are examples concrete?
4. Does the visual make the idea easier, or merely decorate it?
5. Can the audience repeat the conclusion afterward?

Use the [Storyboard](/studio/storyboard) for the reasoning and [Studio](/studio) for timing, type, diagrams, and animation.
