---
title: "Create a motion graphic"
description: "Make a short, motion-led title, statistic, chart, logo sting, or overlay."
---

Use Motion Graphics when the piece is short, usually under ten seconds, and movement carries the message.

Good fits include:

- kinetic type;
- one important number;
- a chart reveal;
- a logo sting;
- a lower third;
- an animated quote, headline, or post;
- a transparent overlay for another video.

## Example request

```text
/hyperframes Create a six-second transparent overlay for “2.4× faster”.
The number should count up, settle firmly, then clear cleanly. No narration.
Use white type with one lime accent and export for editing over footage.
```

Include the exact words or data, intended background, dimensions, duration, and whether transparency is required.

If the piece needs several scenes, narration, or a longer story, use the General Video workflow instead.

## Approve the motion idea

Confirm:

- the one message or number;
- the exact duration and dimensions;
- where the graphic will be used;
- the background or transparency requirement;
- the entrance, main action, and clean exit;
- whether the first or final frame must hold.

Review the first and final frames carefully. An overlay must enter and leave cleanly when placed over another edit.

## Common problems

| Problem | Better direction |
| --- | --- |
| Too many ideas compete in six seconds | Keep one message and one motion idea. |
| Text is readable only while paused | Hold the resolved state longer. |
| A transparent overlay leaves visible debris | Review the first and final frames over real footage. |
| Motion is energetic but communicates nothing | Tie the movement to the meaning of the word, number, or chart. |
| The result needs narration and several scenes | Move to the custom video workflow. |
