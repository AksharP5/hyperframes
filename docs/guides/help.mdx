## What to include when asking for help

- What you were trying to do.
- The command or visible action you took.
- The exact error or unexpected result.
- Whether it happens every time.
- Your operating system and HyperFrames version.
- A screenshot or short recording when the problem is visual.
- A small reproducible project when the source can be shared.

Do not paste secrets, access tokens, private media, or credentials into a public issue.
