---
title: "Edit captions"
description: "Review caption text, timing, styling, and animation in Studio."
---

Captions should help people follow the spoken content without competing with the video.

## Review the words first

Correct transcription mistakes, names, punctuation, and line breaks before polishing the animation.

Listen to the audio while checking the caption timeline. A visually neat caption that appears on the wrong word is still wrong.

## Adjust timing

Move or trim caption segments so the text follows the speech. Keep enough words together to make sense, but avoid leaving a large paragraph on screen.

## Adjust the design

Choose type, size, placement, contrast, and emphasis that remain readable across the footage.

Use a restrained style for normal subtitles. Use expressive word-level animation when the caption itself is part of the visual storytelling.

Browse [caption components](/catalog/components/caption-highlight) for reusable treatments.

## Check the complete video

- Watch with sound.
- Watch once with sound muted.
- Check important words against cuts and overlays.
- Check the caption position over every background.
- Confirm the final caption disappears cleanly.
